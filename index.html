Frequently asked questions

Are your listed properties RERA approved?
All new projects we list carry a valid TNRERA registration number, shown on the listing. You can verify it yourself on the Tamil Nadu RERA website. Resale properties are checked for title, EC and approvals instead.
Are there any hidden charges or brokerage?
No hidden charges. Our brokerage is told to you upfront, in writing.
Can you help me get a home loan?
Yes, free of cost. We compare offers from partner banks, help with eligibility and handle the paperwork. Most of our listed projects are pre-approved by major banks.
Is the booking amount refundable?
It depends on the builder's or seller's terms, which we share in writing before you pay. Always check cancellation terms in the agreement — we'll walk you through them.
Can I visit the site? Is pickup available?
Yes — site visits are free, including weekends. Pickup and drop can be arranged within city limits.
Can you help me sell my property?
Yes. We give you a free market valuation, market your property to pre-qualified buyers, and handle the paperwork through to registration.
Is buying property here a good investment?
It depends on location, price and your time horizon. We give you facts — infrastructure plans, demand in the area and past price trends — so you can decide. We never promise guaranteed returns.
